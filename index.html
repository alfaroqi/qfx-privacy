Privacy Policy

qfx · Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how qfx ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, and protects data when the app connects to an Instagram account through Meta's Instagram API.

1. Purpose of the app

qfx is used to receive notifications (webhooks) when a connected Instagram Business or Creator account is @mentioned in a comment or caption, so the account owner does not need to check the Instagram app manually.

2. Data we collect

The data we receive and process is limited to:

  • The ID and username of the connected Instagram account.
  • The comment ID and media ID where a mention occurred.
  • The text of the comment or caption that mentions the account, and the username of the person who wrote it, when retrieved through the Instagram API.
  • The access token granted by the account owner to perform the functions above.

We do not collect Instagram or Facebook passwords, and we do not access direct messages unless that permission is explicitly granted for a specific feature.

3. How we use data

  • To deliver or forward mention notifications to the account owner.
  • To keep a record of mentions so they are not processed twice.
  • To maintain the security and stability of the service.

We do not sell user data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with third parties, except where required by law.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on servers that we manage and is accessible only to authorized personnel. Access tokens are stored separately and protected against unauthorized access. We take reasonable measures to protect data, but no system is completely risk-free.

5. Data retention

Mention data is retained only as long as needed to operate the app, and is deleted when the account owner requests deletion or disconnects the app.

6. Data deletion

You can request deletion of all your data as follows:

  1. Send an email to qifani35 with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include your Instagram username.
  2. We will delete the related data within 30 days at the latest and confirm by email.

You can also revoke the app's access at any time in Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites, then remove qfx from the list of active apps.

7. Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data that we hold. Please contact us using the details below.

8. Third-party services

The app uses the Instagram API and Meta platforms. Use of those services is subject to the Instagram Privacy Policy and Meta's terms.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to qifani35.
